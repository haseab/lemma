QUESTIONS

Common questions.

Is Lemma free?+

Yes. Lemma is free and open source under the MIT license. There’s no account, paid tier, or subscription.

Can I write ordinary text too?+

Yes. The desktop app starts in math and recognizes prose as you type. You can also choose Math or Text mode, and change fonts, sizes, and colors. The interactive preview above demonstrates equation editing.

Where does my work go?+

The desktop app saves your pages, undo history, and automatic backups locally on your computer. It doesn’t send your notebook to a server. The web demo stays in this tab and isn’t saved when you leave.

Does it work with LaTeX?+

Import supported LaTeX equations or edit their source directly. Export your whole page as a .tex document, a PDF, plain text, or an editable .lemma file. Custom TeX macros and full document layouts aren’t supported.

What’s a .lemma file?+

It’s an editable copy of one page, including its title, equations, prose, formatting, and input mode. Share it with someone using Lemma or keep it as a backup. Exported pages don’t include local undo history.

Is this ready for my daily work?+

Lemma is an early preview. Save an exported copy of important work, and check the release notes for platform support and known limitations.
